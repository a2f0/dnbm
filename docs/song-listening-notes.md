# Song audition notes

These are score-level changes and audition cues, **not listening verdicts**. No audio
was auditioned while authoring this pass. Pattern tests establish arrangement facts;
they cannot establish fidelity, musical quality, masking or comfortable loudness.

All eleven titles, tempos, swing settings and arranged bar counts are retained.
The principal bass phrases now exchange sub and mid-bass calls with explicit rests;
selected unisons and the heavier existing variations remain. Main grooves use quiet,
normal and accented hats. Shared hat choke groups no longer receive simultaneous
closed/open triggers. Every song has a distinct transition and return, rather than
getting the same fill or a master-level increase. The transitions release both bass
gates for their last beat; release envelopes and effects can still ring across it.

Bar numbers below are one-based, from the start of the arrangement.

## Listening still required

Compare the original and revised **whole-song arcs**, plus matched excerpts of each
main groove and the transition/return above, at matched playback level. Check bass
release tails, kick/sub separation, ghost-snare audibility, hat harshness, mono
translation and the final loop into the intro. In particular, do not reward extra
notes over the deliberate spaces in Bathyal, Mire, Oubliette and Wraith. Revisit a
phrase or timbre if listening contradicts the score-level intent.
